      How these numbers are worked out
      • Money given out: loans whose Money given date is in these dates.
      • Collected: what customers paid on loans repaid in these dates.
      • Interest collected: collected, minus the money that was given on those same loans. Interest is counted when it is paid.
      • Profit: interest collected, minus expenses, minus losses.
      • Interest expected: the interest on loans still out on the last day. It is not profit yet.
      • Overdue right now: the total to repay on loans that are overdue today.
      • Net cash: collected, minus money given out, minus expenses.
